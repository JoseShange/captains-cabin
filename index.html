The Cabin

Fifty metres
from the bay

Captain's Cabin sits on Main Road in Saldanha, a short walk from the water — close enough that the harbour is part of the view and the seafood does not travel far to reach the plate.

It works two shifts. Through the day it is a family restaurant: seafood, sushi, grills and curries, coffee on the deck, kids at the long wooden tables. After dark it turns into a cocktail and sports bar, with the Tiki-Bar open, the big screens on and live music on the calendar.

There is rooftop seating upstairs, tables out on the deck and the bay across the road — which is why so many people end up staying a lot longer than they planned.

It has become one of the West Coast's natural gathering points — bike clubs on a Sunday run, birthday tables that spill across the room, locals who have been coming for years and holidaymakers who find it once and come back.
